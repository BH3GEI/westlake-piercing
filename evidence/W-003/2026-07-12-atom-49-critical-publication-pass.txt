This confirms the repaired path: `RegisterNative` stages the real critical pointer while
the class is not visibly initialized; `InterpreterJni` retrieves that pending pointer
before dlsym lookup; the critical call mutates and reads the real `Paint` flags. It is
not a static stub or a no-op marker.
