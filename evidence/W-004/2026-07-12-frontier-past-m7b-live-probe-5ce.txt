INFRA WIN — the native probe .so builds on THIS MAC (no WSL/Windows)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
build-dlopen-probe.ps1 uses WSL aarch64-linux-gnu-gcc. It is NOT needed: brew
`musl-cross` ships aarch64-linux-musl-gcc, and the build is freestanding, so:
    /opt/homebrew/opt/musl-cross/bin/aarch64-linux-musl-gcc \
      -fPIC -shared -nostdlib -nodefaultlibs -fno-stack-protector \
      -I/opt/homebrew/opt/openjdk/include \
      -Wl,--unresolved-symbols=ignore-all \
      -Wl,-soname,libwestlake_embedded_art_dlopen_probe.so \
      -o out.so westlake_embedded_art_dlopen_probe.c
Reproduced codex's baseline .so as a valid "ELF 64-bit LSB shared object, ARM aarch64"
(142152 B) with correct JNI exports (Java_android_os_Trace_native*, westlake_*). musl is
actually apt here (OH is musl-based) and -nostdlib links no libc. This removes the
Windows/WSL dependency for every future .so rebuild — do it natively on the Mac.
