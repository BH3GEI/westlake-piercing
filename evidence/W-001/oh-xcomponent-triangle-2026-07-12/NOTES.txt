OH-native XComponent app v2 — rotating GLES2 triangle — on 5ce panel — 2026-07-12
================================================================================
A second, richer OH-native .hap (com.westlake.glxc2, "西湖 · GLES2 旋转三角"),
built + signed + deployed on board 5ce (uis7885 / OH 6.1.0.31), rendering LIVE on
the physical panel. Step-up from the v1 glClearColor smoke (com.westlake.glxc):

WHAT'S NEW vs v1
- Real geometry through a compiled GLES2 program: interleaved VBO (x,y,r,g,b),
  vertex shader with a rotation matrix (uAngle) + aspect squish (uAspect),
  fragment shader interpolating per-vertex colour. A per-vertex-coloured triangle
  (red apex / green / blue) spins ~1 full turn / 5s at 60fps over a pulsing
  dark-navy background that never reaches black.
- ArkUI app chrome: Column with a header Text ("西湖 · GLES2 旋转三角") and footer
  ("rotating triangle · OH-native rail · 5ce panel") around the XComponent, on a
  0xFF0B1020 background — so the app has visible ArkUI text chrome, not just a
  full-bleed GL canvas.
- ROBUSTNESS the v1 smoke lacked: eglSwapBuffers is error-checked; on failure the
  EGLSurface is torn down and REBUILT from the still-valid OHNativeWindow
  (self-heal after a focus/window change invalidates the surface). v1 had no swap
  error handling, so once its surface went stale it swapped into the void forever
  and the panel overlay stuck black while the loop kept logging "frames".

RAIL (unchanged, == the OH-native Tuanjie path)
  ArkUI <XComponent type:SURFACE libraryname:'entry'> → libentry.so napi Init →
  OnSurfaceCreated hands OHNativeWindow → EGL/GLES2 program draws → eglSwapBuffers
  → render_service → composer_host → 5ce panel.
